Floating label fields

A labelled field keeps its label inside the field while it is empty and unfocused, then rises it above the field on focus or once it has content. Try clicking or typing into the fields.

Field values



            
Markup

            
<div class="fw-floating-field">
    <label for="title">Title of publication</label>
    <input type="text" id="title" placeholder=" ">
</div>

<script type="module">
    import {initFloatingLabels} from "fwtoolkit"
    initFloatingLabels()
</script>
